Shortcuts

Each action can have multiple keys. Click + Add key then press the combination; click × on a key to remove it. A key can only belong to one action. Shortcuts work while a YouTube tab is focused. Press Esc while recording to cancel.

Global hotkeys (optional local app)

The extension works fully on its own; shortcuts above act on the focused YouTube tab. To control playback while other windows are focused, run the companion app (see local-app/ in the repo). It registers system-wide hotkeys and forwards them to the extension over a loopback connection. Hotkey mappings are configured in the app itself.
